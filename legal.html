Terms, privacy and cookies

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version: the test is free, your result is an opinion and not a diagnosis, and nothing about you is collected unless you choose to share it at the end.

Terms of use

EthosScales is a free political values test. By taking it you agree to these terms.

  • It is a model, not a measurement. Your result is an estimate made from your answers. The positions given to ideologies and parties are editorial judgements. Do not treat the result as professional, legal or academic advice.
  • Describing is not endorsing. The site describes many ideologies and parties, including extreme ones, from their own founding texts. Listing one does not support it.
  • Use it fairly. Do not send fake results, flood the site with automated requests, or try to break it.
  • No guarantee. The site is provided as it is. It may change, contain mistakes or go offline.
  • The code is open. It is published under the MIT License. Portraits have their own licences, listed in the credits.

Spotted a mistake? Suggest a correction.

Privacy

What happens if you just take the test

Your answers are scored inside your own browser. Nothing is sent to us. Your result is written into the page address so you can share the link, and anyone you send that link to can see the thirteen scores in it.

What happens if you choose to share

At the end of the test you are asked whether you want to help with our statistics. Only if you press the button that says you want to share, one anonymous row is saved. It holds:

  • your thirteen scores, your closest ideology and which length of test you took
  • whichever of these you chose to answer: country, age group, gender, religion, and the five belief questions
  • the time the row was saved

It does not hold your name, email address, IP address, exact age or anything that says who you are. Every question is optional.

Why we ask

To see trends, such as how results differ between countries or age groups, to publish summaries of those trends, and to find statements in the test that work badly. Rows are only ever used and published in bulk, never one person at a time. The data is never sold.

Sensitive answers and your consent

Political views and religion are sensitive kinds of information. We only save them with your clear consent, which you give by pressing the share button. You can skip the questions and still get your full result.

Age

If you say you are under 13, nothing is saved. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian if you are unsure about sharing.

Where it is kept and for how long

Shared rows are stored in a private Google Sheet, on Google's servers, that only the person who runs EthosScales can open. Rows are kept for as long as the project runs.

Your choices

Because rows are anonymous, we usually cannot tell which one is yours. If you want a row removed, open an issue on GitHub with your exact thirteen scores and the day you took the test, and we will delete any row that matches.

Cookies and storage

EthosScales sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for three things, all of which stay on your device:

  • to remember that you accepted these terms
  • to save your place if you leave part way through the test
  • to remember that you already shared a result, so it is not counted twice

You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

Other services the site loads

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts and two code libraries from public content networks (cdnjs and Plotly). Like any website, these services see your IP address when your browser asks them for a file. If you choose to share, your row is sent to Google, which runs the sheet it is saved in.

Contact

Questions about any of this can be raised as an issue on GitHub.
