What is Conservative Revolution?

Conservative Revolution is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. Moeller treated liberalism as the force that dissolves peoples and the party state of Weimar as its German form. He called for a new Reich that would end the division into parties and classes, joining a nationalism purged of the old monarchy with a German socialism purged of Marx. Conservatism for him meant creating things worth conserving, not restoring the past. He criticised Hitler and died in 1925, but the National Socialists took the title of his book as a slogan.

Against liberalism and party rule, a new Reich above the parties.

Defined from: Arthur Moeller van den Bruck, Das Dritte Reich (The Third Reich) (1923)
