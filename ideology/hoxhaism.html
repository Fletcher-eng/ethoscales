What is Hoxhaism?

Hoxhaism is a political ideology in the Radical Left family. Written as Albania broke with China, the book holds that the United States and the Soviet Union are both imperialist powers and that Maoism, Titoism, Khrushchev's course and Eurocommunism are all departures from Marxism Leninism. It defends the line of Lenin and Stalin as the only correct one, rejects the Chinese theory of three worlds, and insists that a socialist country must rely on its own forces and refuse dependence on any great power. Rule by a single vanguard party and a fully state owned, planned economy are treated as beyond question.

Marxism Leninism defended against every revision, Soviet, Yugoslav and Chinese alike.

Defined from: Enver Hoxha, Imperialism and the Revolution (1978)
