What is Thomism?

Thomism is a political ideology in the Religious family. Aquinas began from Aristotle: people are social and political by nature, so government is natural and not merely a remedy for sin. A community needs one directing power to seek the common good, and rule by one just king is best because it best secures unity and peace. Rule for private gain is tyranny, the worst of regimes, to be checked by public authority and not by private violence. Since the final end of human life is beyond this world, kings are subject in spiritual matters to the priesthood.

Government exists for the common good.

Defined from: Thomas Aquinas, On Kingship (De Regno) (c. 1265 to 1267)
