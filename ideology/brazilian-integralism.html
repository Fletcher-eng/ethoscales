What is Brazilian Integralism?

Brazilian Integralism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. It calls for an integral state that unites every class and region of Brazil under a spiritual conception of life. It is corporatist and anti liberal like European fascism, but its official line rejects racial doctrine and presents the nation as a fusion of all its peoples, although the movement also had a strongly antisemitic wing. Christian faith is treated as the foundation of the whole political order.

God, fatherland, family.

Defined from: Plínio Salgado, The October Manifesto (1932)
