What is Post Left Anarchism?

Post Left Anarchism is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. The earlier essay argues that work, defined as compulsory production, is the source of most misery and should be abolished in favour of free and playful activity. Anarchy after Leftism is a reply to Murray Bookchin, who had attacked what he called lifestyle anarchism. Black argues that Bookchin's programme of municipal assemblies and majority rule is a form of government, and that anarchism should cut its ties to the Left, with its organisations, its moralism and its respect for labour and industry, and begin from individual autonomy.

Anarchy without the Left, without ideology and without work.

Defined from: Bob Black, The Abolition of Work (1985) and Anarchy after Leftism (1997)
