What is Kantian Republicanism?

Kantian Republicanism is a political ideology in the Liberal family. Kant sets out the articles of a lasting peace. Every state should have a republican constitution, meaning representative government under law with the executive separated from the legislature, which he distinguishes from direct democracy. States should join a federation of free states, not a single world state, and standing armies should in time be abolished. Every person has a cosmopolitan right to visit foreign lands without being treated as an enemy.

Republics joined in a federation of free states can end war.

Defined from: Immanuel Kant, Perpetual Peace: A Philosophical Sketch (1795)
