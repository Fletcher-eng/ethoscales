What is Communization Theory?

Communization Theory is a political ideology in the Autonomist family. Coming out of the French ultra left after May 1968, Dauvé argues that communism is not a programme to be applied after workers take power. A revolution is communist only if it begins at once to abolish wage labour, money, exchange and the state, and with them the working class as a class. On this view, workers managing their own factories would only be managing capital. He is therefore wary of parties, unions and councils alike when they are treated as forms that could guarantee the outcome.

Communism as the immediate act of revolution, not a stage that follows it.

Defined from: Gilles Dauvé (as Jean Barrot) and François Martin, Eclipse and Re-emergence of the Communist Movement (1974)
