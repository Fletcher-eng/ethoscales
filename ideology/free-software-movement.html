What is Free Software Movement?

Free Software Movement is a political ideology in the Technological family. Stallman announces a complete operating system that anyone may copy, modify and pass on, and asks programmers to help build it. He argues that sharing software is a basic act of friendship among programmers, and that owners who forbid copying and hide source code set users against one another and waste effort through duplication. Programmers can still earn a living through support, teaching and custom work. Copies may be changed and redistributed, but nobody may restrict their further redistribution.

Software should be free to use, study, share and change.

Defined from: Richard Stallman, The GNU Manifesto (1985)
