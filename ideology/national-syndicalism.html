What is National Syndicalism?

National Syndicalism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. Ledesma fused the revolutionary unions of syndicalism with radical nationalism, before Falangism absorbed his movement. The whole economy is to be organised into compulsory state syndicates, with big wealth subordinated to national ends. It is more secular, more worker focused and more openly revolutionary than the Catholic Falangism it merged into.

The syndicates of the left, the nation of the right.

Defined from: Ramiro Ledesma Ramos, La Conquista del Estado manifesto (1931)
