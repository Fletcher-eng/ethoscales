What is Compassionate Conservatism?

Compassionate Conservatism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. Olasky's history of American poor relief argues that earlier charity worked because it was personal: givers knew the people they helped, expected effort in return and addressed spiritual as well as material need. He holds that government welfare programmes replaced this with impersonal entitlements and made poverty worse. Care for the poor is a duty, but it is best carried out by churches, voluntary groups and individuals, not by state bureaucracies.

Help for the poor should be personal, challenging and spiritual.

Defined from: Marvin Olasky, The Tragedy of American Compassion (1992)
