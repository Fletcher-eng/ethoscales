What is Political Augustinianism?

Political Augustinianism is a political ideology in the Religious family. Written after the sack of Rome, the book divides humanity into two cities: one formed by love of God, the other by love of self and the lust to dominate. No earthly state is the city of God, and a kingdom without justice differs from a robber band only in scale. Government exists because of sin, to restrain wrongdoing and keep a limited peace, which believers should use and obey as pilgrims passing through. War may be just, but it is always a matter for grief.

Two cities, formed by two loves.

Defined from: Augustine of Hippo, The City of God (c. 413 to 426)
