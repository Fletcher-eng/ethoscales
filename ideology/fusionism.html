What is Fusionism?

Fusionism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. Meyer set out to join the libertarian and traditionalist wings of American conservatism. Virtue is the highest aim of a human life, but an act is only virtuous if it is freely chosen, so the state cannot make people good. Government should therefore be confined to defence, order and the administration of justice, leaving the economy to the free market and morals to persons, families and churches. He paired this with firm opposition to Communism.

Freedom is the end of politics, virtue the end of man.

Defined from: Frank S. Meyer, In Defense of Freedom: A Conservative Credo (1962)
