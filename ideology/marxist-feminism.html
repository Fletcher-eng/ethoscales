What is Marxist Feminism?

Marxist Feminism is a political ideology in the Left family. Kollontai argued that there is no single woman question shared by all women, because women are divided by class as men are. Equal legal rights within capitalism would mainly benefit women of the propertied classes, so working women should organise with working men and not with liberal feminists. Real freedom requires economic independence and a society that takes over housework and the care of children as public tasks. Marriage would then rest on affection alone and not on property or dependence.

Women's liberation through the liberation of labour.

Defined from: Alexandra Kollontai, The Social Basis of the Woman Question (1909)
