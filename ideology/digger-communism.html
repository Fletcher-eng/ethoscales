What is Digger Communism?

Digger Communism is a political ideology in the Classical family. Addressed to Oliver Cromwell after the Digger colonies had been broken up, the book sets out a commonwealth in which land is held in common and buying, selling and working for hire are abolished. Each household keeps its own home and goods, works at farming or a trade, and draws what it needs from public storehouses. All officers, up to parliament, are chosen every year by the men of the commonwealth. Winstanley argued from scripture that true freedom is free use of the earth, and he attacked landlords, lawyers and a paid clergy.
