What is Archeofuturism?

Archeofuturism is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. Faye predicted that modern egalitarian civilisation would collapse under converging crises. What follows should combine pre modern values, such as hierarchy, rootedness and a warrior ethic, with the most advanced science and technology. He imagined a federated European empire with a high tech elite and a neo traditional majority.

Archaic values, futuristic technology.

Defined from: Guillaume Faye, Archeofuturism (1998)
