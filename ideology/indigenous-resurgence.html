What is Indigenous Resurgence?

Indigenous Resurgence is a political ideology in the Autonomist family. Alfred, a Kanien'kehá:ka (Mohawk) scholar, argues that Native communities in North America will not recover by winning a share of state power. Band councils and self government agreements, in his account, draw leaders into the colonial system, and even sovereignty is a European idea that does not fit indigenous political thought. He calls for a return to traditional values and teachings, leadership that is accountable and works by consensus, and a new generation committed to defending nationhood and the land.

Self government on indigenous terms, drawn from tradition and not from the state.

Defined from: Taiaiake Alfred, Peace, Power, Righteousness: An Indigenous Manifesto (1999)
