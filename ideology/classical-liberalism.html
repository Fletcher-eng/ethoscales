What is Classical Liberalism?

Classical Liberalism is a political ideology in the Liberal family. Mill's harm principle: society may restrict a person only to stop them harming someone else, never for their own good. Freedom of thought, speech and ways of living must be protected even against the majority. Government should be representative and limited.

Power may only be used over someone to prevent harm to others.

Defined from: John Stuart Mill, On Liberty (1859)
