What is Strasserism?

Strasserism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. The anti capitalist wing that broke with Hitler in 1930. Strasser wanted large estates broken up and large industry held in shared tenure by the state, the workers and managers, through a system of entailed fiefs. He proposed a federal Germany of self governing regions and professional estates, and a return of much of the population to the land, while remaining intensely nationalist.

A national socialism turned against big capital.

Defined from: Otto Strasser, Germany Tomorrow (1940)
