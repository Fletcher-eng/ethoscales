What is Collectivist Anarchism?

Collectivist Anarchism is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. Bakunin's last major work argues that every state, whatever it calls itself, means the rule of a minority over the mass of the people. A government of workers' representatives or of socialist experts, as he says the followers of Marx propose, would become a new privileged class. The alternative is a social revolution made by workers and peasants themselves, which destroys the state and builds society from below as a free federation of associations and communes holding land and tools in common.

No state can free the people, not even a workers' state.

Defined from: Mikhail Bakunin, Statism and Anarchy (1873)
