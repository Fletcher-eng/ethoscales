What is Postliberalism?

Postliberalism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. Deneen argues that liberalism is failing by its own success. By defining freedom as release from custom, place and nature, it dissolves family, community and religion, and the isolated individual who remains depends on an ever larger state and market. Left and right liberalism are two wings of one project. He proposes no new ideology but the patient rebuilding of local communities, household economies and shared culture, from which a better theory may later grow.

Liberalism has failed because it has succeeded.

Defined from: Patrick J. Deneen, Why Liberalism Failed (2018)
