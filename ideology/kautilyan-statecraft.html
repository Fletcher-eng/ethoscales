What is Kautilyan Statecraft?

Kautilyan Statecraft is a political ideology in the Classical family. A manual for a king. The state has seven limbs, from ruler and ministers to treasury, army and allies, and the king's own good lies in the welfare of his subjects. He rules through a large administration that runs mines, trade and farms, fixes penalties in detail and watches officials and people through networks of spies. Abroad, every neighbour is a natural enemy and the neighbour's neighbour a natural ally, and the king chooses between peace, war, waiting and intrigue by cold advantage. Scholars now date the text well after the minister it is named for.

The science of acquiring and keeping the earth.

Defined from: Attributed to Kautilya, Arthashastra (c. 2nd century BC to 3rd century AD)
