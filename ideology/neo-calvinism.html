What is Neo Calvinism (Sphere Sovereignty)?

Neo Calvinism (Sphere Sovereignty) is a political ideology in the Religious family. In his address opening the Free University of Amsterdam, Kuyper argued that absolute sovereignty belongs to God alone and is delegated separately to the distinct spheres of life: family, church, science, business, art and the state. No sphere may swallow another. The state has the task of keeping the boundaries between them and protecting the weak within them, but it may not run them. From this he drew the case for schools, universities and associations free of both state and established church.

Each sphere of life answers to God, not to the state.

Defined from: Abraham Kuyper, Sphere Sovereignty (1880)
