What is Bolivarianism?

Bolivarianism is a political ideology in the Nationalist family. In the Jamaica Letter Bolívar defended independence from Spain and hoped the new American nations would be bound together in a league, meeting in a congress at Panama. At Angostura he argued that laws must fit the people they govern, and that federalism copied from the United States had weakened Venezuela. He proposed a unitary republic with a strong president, a hereditary senate, a branch of government for public morals and education, and the abolition of slavery.

Independence, union and a strong republic for Spanish America.

Defined from: Simón Bolívar, The Jamaica Letter (1815) and the Angostura Address (1819)
