What is Social Ecology (Communalism)?

Social Ecology (Communalism) is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. It argues that ecological destruction comes from social hierarchy, so it cannot be solved without ending hierarchy. Bookchin proposed libertarian municipalism: face to face citizens' assemblies that own the local economy and confederate with each other. Technology is welcome when it is humanly scaled and liberates people from toil.

The domination of nature begins with the domination of people.

Defined from: Murray Bookchin, The Ecology of Freedom (1982)
