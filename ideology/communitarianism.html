What is Communitarianism?

Communitarianism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. It argues that liberal societies have overemphasised individual rights and neglected the communities that make free people possible. Families, schools and neighbourhoods carry moral values and need active support. It seeks a balance between personal liberty and the common good, enforced mostly by social pressure and not by the state.

Strong rights presume strong responsibilities.

Defined from: Amitai Etzioni, The Spirit of Community (1993)
