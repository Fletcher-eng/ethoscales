What is Agrarianism?

Agrarianism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. Berry argues that industrial agriculture drove families off the land and treats soil as a mine to be exhausted, guided by an exploiter's mind that asks only how much can be taken. Against it he sets the nurturer, who asks what a place needs to stay healthy. The health of land, body, household and community cannot be separated. He calls for small, diverse family farms, local markets, tools on a human scale, and limits on the power of agribusiness and its experts.

Good farming, settled households and local economies are the basis of culture.

Defined from: Wendell Berry, The Unsettling of America: Culture and Agriculture (1977)
