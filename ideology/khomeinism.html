What is Khomeinism?

Khomeinism is a political ideology in the Religious family. Khomeini argued that in the absence of the hidden Imam, the right to govern passes to just and learned Islamic jurists. Monarchy is illegitimate. Government exists to enforce divine law. In the Islamic Republic that followed, this became a Supreme Leader with final authority over every other institution, including elected ones. It also frames politics as the oppressed against the arrogant powers.

Guardianship of the jurist.

Defined from: Ruhollah Khomeini, Islamic Government: Governance of the Jurist (1970)
