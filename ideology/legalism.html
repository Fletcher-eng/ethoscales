What is Legalism?

Legalism is a political ideology in the Classical family. Han Fei held that people follow their own interest, so a state built on the virtue of rulers or subjects is built on sand. Order comes from clear public laws applied to high and low alike, enforced by the two handles of reward and punishment. The ruler keeps his position by holding these himself, hiding his intentions, and judging ministers strictly by whether results match their words. Farming and war make the state strong; scholars who praise the past, private swordsmen and merchants weaken it and should be curbed.

Rule by law, reward and punishment, not by virtue.

Defined from: Han Fei, Han Feizi (mid 3rd century BC)
