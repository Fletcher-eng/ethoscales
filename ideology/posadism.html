What is Posadism?

Posadism is a political ideology in the Meme family. A real Trotskyist current that the internet adopted as a meme. Posadas argued that nuclear war was inevitable and that the workers' states should not fear it, since socialism would be built on the ruins. He also reasoned that any civilisation able to cross space must already be socialist, so visiting aliens would be allies.

Nuclear war, then socialism, then the aliens.

Defined from: J. Posadas, Flying Saucers, the Process of Matter and Energy, Science, the Revolutionary and Working Class Struggle and the Socialist Future of Mankind (1968)
