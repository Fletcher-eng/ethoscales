What is Keynesian Liberalism?

Keynesian Liberalism is a political ideology in the Liberal family. Keynes showed that a market economy can settle at high unemployment for long periods, because total demand may fall short. Government should manage demand through spending, taxation and interest rates. The aim is to save capitalism and individual liberty by correcting their worst failure, not to replace them.

Markets can get stuck, and the state can unstick them.

Defined from: John Maynard Keynes, The General Theory of Employment, Interest and Money (1936)
