What is Italian Fascism?

Italian Fascism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. It holds that the individual has meaning only through the nation, and the nation only through the state, which is an ethical and spiritual whole and not a referee between interests. Property stays private but must serve national ends, with employers and workers bound together in state corporations. It rejects liberal individualism, Marxist class struggle and pacifism alike, and treats struggle and war as what brings a people to its full strength.

Everything within the state, nothing outside the state, nothing against the state.

Defined from: Giovanni Gentile and Benito Mussolini, The Doctrine of Fascism (1932)
