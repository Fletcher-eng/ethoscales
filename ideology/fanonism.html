What is Fanonism?

Fanonism is a political ideology in the Left family. Written during the Algerian war. Fanon argued that colonial rule is founded on violence and divides the world into two, so it can only be ended by the armed struggle of the colonised, which also frees them from the sense of inferiority that colonialism instils. He looked to the peasantry and the urban poor as the revolutionary force. He warned that after independence a national middle class would step into the settlers' place unless national consciousness grew into social and political consciousness, with power spread out among the people.

Decolonisation as the remaking of a people through their own struggle.

Defined from: Frantz Fanon, The Wretched of the Earth (1961)
