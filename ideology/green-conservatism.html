What is Green Conservatism?

Green Conservatism is a political ideology in the Green family. Scruton argued that environmental problems arise when people can pass the costs of their actions on to others and to future generations, and that conservatism, as the politics of stewardship and inheritance, is well placed to answer them. The motive that makes people accept sacrifices is love of home, an attachment to a place shared by the dead, the living and the unborn. He therefore favoured local associations, civic initiative, legal liability and the nation state over international treaties, large campaigning organisations and central regulation.

Care for the environment grows from love of home.

Defined from: Roger Scruton, Green Philosophy: How to Think Seriously About the Planet (2012)
