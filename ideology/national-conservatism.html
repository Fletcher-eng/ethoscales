What is National Conservatism?

National Conservatism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. It argues that the best political order is one of free and independent national states, each ruling itself by its own traditions. It opposes both liberal internationalism and empire as attempts to impose one law on all peoples. A nation is held together by mutual loyalty, shared language, religion and history, not by abstract rights alone.

A world of independent nations, not a universal order.

Defined from: Yoram Hazony, The Virtue of Nationalism (2018)
