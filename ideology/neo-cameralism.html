What is Neo Cameralism?

Neo Cameralism is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. It treats a state as a business that owns a country. It should be run for profit by a chief executive who answers to shareholders, with no elections. Yarvin imagines a patchwork of many small sovereign corporations competing for residents, whose main protection is the freedom to leave.

The state as a company, the ruler as its chief executive.

Defined from: Curtis Yarvin, writing as Mencius Moldbug, Patchwork: A Political System for the 21st Century (2008)
