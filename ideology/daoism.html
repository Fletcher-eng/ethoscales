What is Daoism?

Daoism is a political ideology in the Classical family. Read as a book of government, the Tao Te Ching advises the ruler to act as little as possible and let things follow their natural course. The more laws and prohibitions there are, the poorer and more unruly the people become; the best ruler is one whose subjects barely know he exists. Weapons are instruments of ill omen, to be used only when there is no choice. Its ideal is a small country with few people, who have labour saving tools but do not use them and are content never to visit the next village.

Govern by not interfering.

Defined from: Attributed to Laozi, Tao Te Ching (c. 4th century BC)
