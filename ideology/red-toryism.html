What is Red Toryism?

Red Toryism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. Blond argues that the social liberalism of the left and the economic liberalism of the right worked together to break up civil society, leaving isolated individuals facing a centralised state and monopolised markets. He calls for a moral market, a local economy and wide ownership of assets among the poor. Public services should pass to mutuals and associations run by their workers and users, and power should return to towns and neighbourhoods.

Against the monopoly of the state and the monopoly of the market.

Defined from: Phillip Blond, Red Tory: How Left and Right Have Broken Britain and How We Can Fix It (2010)
