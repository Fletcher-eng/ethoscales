What is Effective Accelerationism?

Effective Accelerationism is a political ideology in the Technological family. It treats the growth of intelligence and energy use as a law of the universe that should be embraced. Technology and markets are to be pushed forward as fast as possible, especially artificial intelligence. It opposes regulation and safety pauses, and wants development spread widely so that no single authority controls it.

Accelerate, and climb the Kardashev scale.

Defined from: Beff Jezos and bayeslord, Notes on e/acc Principles and Tenets (2022)
