What is Trotskyism?

Trotskyism is a political ideology in the Radical Left family. It argues that in backward countries the working class must lead the revolution straight through to socialism, and that it can only survive by spreading internationally. Socialism in a single country is impossible. Trotsky also condemned the Soviet bureaucracy as a caste that had usurped the workers' state.

The revolution cannot stop at one country.

Defined from: Leon Trotsky, The Permanent Revolution (1930)
