Ideologies / Technological

What is Technocracy?

Technocracy is a political ideology in the Technological family. Argued that politics and the price system could not manage an economy of abundance. Scott proposed replacing both with a continental Technate run by engineers and scientists chosen for competence. Money would be replaced by energy certificates, and production planned as an engineering problem.

Government by engineers, accounting in energy.

Defined from: Technocracy Inc., written chiefly by M. King Hubbert, Technocracy Study Course (1934)
