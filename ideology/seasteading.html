What is Seasteading?

Seasteading is a political ideology in the Libertarian family. Seasteaders argue that government improves slowly because it is costly to leave one country and impossible to start a new one on land that is already claimed. Floating cities in international waters would lower that barrier. If homes and businesses could detach and move to another platform, governments would have to compete for residents as firms compete for customers, and many small experiments in law could be tried at once. The book also presents ocean settlement as a source of food, clean energy and environmental repair.

Build new societies on the ocean and let governments compete for citizens.

Defined from: Joe Quirk with Patri Friedman, Seasteading: How Floating Nations Will Restore the Environment, Enrich the Poor, Cure the Sick, and Liberate Humanity from Politicians (2017)
