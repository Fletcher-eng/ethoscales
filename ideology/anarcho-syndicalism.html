What is Anarcho Syndicalism?

Anarcho Syndicalism is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. It sees revolutionary trade unions as the means of struggle today and the structure of the economy tomorrow. Workers take over production directly through the general strike and run it through federated unions. It refuses political parties and the conquest of state power.

The union is both the weapon and the future society.

Defined from: Rudolf Rocker, Anarcho Syndicalism: Theory and Practice (1938)
