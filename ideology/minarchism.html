What is Minarchist Libertarianism?

Minarchist Libertarianism is a political ideology in the Libertarian family. It starts from individual rights so strong that they limit what any state may do. Only a minimal state that protects against force, theft and fraud and enforces contracts can be justified. Anything more, including taxing some people to help others, violates rights.

The night watchman state, and nothing more.

Defined from: Robert Nozick, Anarchy, State, and Utopia (1974)
