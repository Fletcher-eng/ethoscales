What is Futarchy?

Futarchy is a political ideology in the Technological family. Hanson argues that democracies fail mainly because they are poor at gathering what is known about which policies work. Under futarchy, elected representatives would still define and measure national welfare, but would no longer choose policies. Speculative markets would estimate welfare with and without each proposed policy, and a proposal would become law when the markets clearly expect it to raise welfare. Those with better information profit by correcting prices, so the system rewards being right.

Vote on values, but bet on beliefs.

Defined from: Robin Hanson, Shall We Vote on Values, But Bet on Beliefs? (2013)
