What is Paleolibertarianism?

Paleolibertarianism is a political ideology in the Libertarian family. Rockwell argues that libertarianism had tied itself to a counterculture hostile to religion, family and bourgeois morals, and that this was both mistaken and off putting to ordinary people. He keeps the full free market and anti state programme but joins it to cultural conservatism. Social authority exercised by families, churches and communities is presented as a natural rival to state power, and Western civilisation and its Judaeo Christian tradition as the ground on which liberty grew.

Against the state, and for the family, the church and inherited culture.

Defined from: Llewellyn H. Rockwell Jr., The Case for Paleo-Libertarianism (1990)
