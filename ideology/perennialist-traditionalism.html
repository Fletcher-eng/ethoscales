What is Perennialist Traditionalism?

Perennialist Traditionalism is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. Guénon held that the modern West is a deviation without precedent, a civilisation built on quantity, matter and the individual and cut off from the metaphysical principles kept by the great traditions. In a normal society spiritual authority, which belongs to those with sacred knowledge, stands above kings and warriors, and disorder begins when temporal power revolts against it. Unlike Evola, he gives primacy to contemplation over action and offers no political programme, only the hope of an intellectual elite that restores tradition.

Spiritual authority above temporal power.

Defined from: René Guénon, The Crisis of the Modern World (1927) and Spiritual Authority and Temporal Power (1929)
