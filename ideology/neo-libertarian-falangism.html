What is Neo Libertarian Falangism?

Neo Libertarian Falangism is a political ideology in the Meme family. A deliberate contradiction. Real Falangism wants a national syndicalist economy with nationalised banking, and real libertarianism rejects the authoritarian nation state. This mash up keeps the Falangist nation, Catholic identity and strongman while swapping the syndicates for free markets and low taxes.
