What is Operaismo (Autonomist Marxism)?

Operaismo (Autonomist Marxism) is a political ideology in the Radical Left family. Tronti turns the usual Marxist order around: it is the struggles of workers that drive capitalist development, forcing capital to reorganise, and not the other way round. Politics should therefore begin from the factory and from the workers' own point of view, which is openly partial and hostile to the system. The decisive weapon is refusal, meaning the refusal of work and of collaboration in production. The book questions how far the established unions and parties still express that antagonism.

Workers' struggle comes first, and capital reorganises itself in response.

Defined from: Mario Tronti, Workers and Capital (1966)
