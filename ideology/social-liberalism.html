What is Social Liberalism?

Social Liberalism is a political ideology in the Liberal family. It asks what rules people would choose if they did not know their own place in society. Rawls answers: equal basic liberties for all first, then inequalities only where they benefit the least well off and positions are open to everyone. This supports markets alongside strong redistribution and public provision.

Justice as fairness.

Defined from: John Rawls, A Theory of Justice (1971)
