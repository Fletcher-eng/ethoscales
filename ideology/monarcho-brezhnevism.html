What is Monarcho Brezhnevism?

Monarcho Brezhnevism is a political ideology in the Meme family. It takes the late Soviet system of the Brezhnev years, with its planned economy, ageing leadership and preference for stability over reform, and makes the top job hereditary. The joke is that the two are closer than they look. If you land here, you scored for full state planning under a ruler nobody elects, with little appetite for change.
