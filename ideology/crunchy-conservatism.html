What is Crunchy Conservatism?

Crunchy Conservatism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. Dreher describes conservatives who live in ways usually linked with the counterculture: organic food, home schooling, old houses, small farms. His manifesto holds that big business deserves as much suspicion as big government, that the care of nature is a conservative duty, and that beauty matters more than efficiency. Culture counts for more than politics or economics, and the family and traditional religious faith are the institutions most worth conserving.

Small, local, old and particular over big, global, new and abstract.

Defined from: Rod Dreher, Crunchy Cons (2006)
