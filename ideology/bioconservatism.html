What is Bioconservatism?

Bioconservatism is a political ideology in the Technological family. Kass argues that cloning, genetic enhancement and the pursuit of ageless bodies threaten the things that give human life its meaning. Our sense of revulsion at such projects holds a wisdom that argument cannot fully state. Biotechnology should therefore be restrained by law in defence of human dignity.

Human nature is not ours to redesign.

Defined from: Leon R. Kass, Life, Liberty, and the Defense of Dignity (2002)
