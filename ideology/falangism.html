Ideologies / Third Position

What is Falangism?

Falangism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. It defines Spain as a unity of destiny that stands above every region, class and party, and rejects separatism outright. The economy is to be organised as one giant syndicate of producers, with banking nationalised and land reformed, so that neither capitalism nor Marxism divides the nation. Unlike Italian Fascism, it places the Catholic sense of life at the centre of national reconstruction.

National syndicalism on a Catholic foundation.

Defined from: José Antonio Primo de Rivera, Falange Española de las JONS, The Twenty Seven Points (1934)
