What is Paleoconservatism?

Paleoconservatism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. The old right that rejects neoconservative foreign policy. A nation should avoid wars and alliances that do not serve its own vital interests, protect its industries with tariffs, and sharply limit immigration to preserve its inherited culture. Power belongs close to home, in states, towns and families.

A republic, not an empire.

Defined from: Patrick J. Buchanan, A Republic, Not an Empire (1999)
