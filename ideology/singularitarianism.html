What is Singularitarianism?

Singularitarianism is a political ideology in the Technological family. Kurzweil argues that information technology improves at an accelerating, exponential rate, and that this will carry genetics, nanotechnology and robotics to a point where machine intelligence exceeds and merges with our own, which he dates to about 2045. Ageing, disease and scarcity could then be overcome, and intelligence would spread outward into the universe. He treats attempts to halt these technologies as futile and harmful, and argues for managing their dangers through defensive technologies and targeted safeguards.

Exponential technology will merge human and machine intelligence within decades.

Defined from: Ray Kurzweil, The Singularity Is Near (2005)
