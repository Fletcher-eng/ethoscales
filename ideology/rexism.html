What is Rexism?

Rexism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. Named after Christ the King, the movement led by Degrelle grew out of Belgian Catholic Action. Denis, its main theorist, presented Rex as a popular movement and not a party, and called for a moral renewal of Belgium through a new hierarchical state organised by corporations, in place of parliamentary parties and the financiers it accused of corrupting them. Under German occupation Degrelle took the movement into full collaboration with National Socialism.

A Catholic and corporatist revolt against the Belgian party system.

Defined from: Jean Denis, Principes Rexistes (1936)
