What is Natural Law Anarchism?

Natural Law Anarchism is a political ideology in the Libertarian family. Spooner, a lawyer and abolitionist, applies the ordinary law of contract to the United States Constitution and finds that it fails. Nobody living signed it, and those who made it had no power to bind later generations. Voting and paying taxes do not show consent, because both are done under threat. A government that takes money without individual consent therefore differs from a highwayman only in claiming to act for its victim's good.

A constitution that nobody signed binds nobody.

Defined from: Lysander Spooner, No Treason, No. VI: The Constitution of No Authority (1870)
