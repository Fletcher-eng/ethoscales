What is Mazzinian Republicanism?

Mazzinian Republicanism is a political ideology in the Nationalist family. Addressed to Italian working men, the book argues that a politics of rights alone breeds selfishness, and that life is a mission built on duties: to humanity first, then to country, then to family. Each nation has a task given by God within the wider association of peoples. Italy must be one free and united republic, with education for all, and with labour and capital joined in the same hands through voluntary association, not through communism.

God and the people.

Defined from: Giuseppe Mazzini, The Duties of Man (1860)
