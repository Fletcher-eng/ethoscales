What is Pirate Politics?

Pirate Politics is a political ideology in the Technological family. Falkvinge founded the Swedish Pirate Party in 2006, and this book sets out its case. Copyright can no longer be enforced against file sharing without monitoring everyone's private communication, so the choice is between that monopoly and basic civil liberties. The authors propose making all non commercial copying and sharing legal, cutting the commercial term to twenty years from publication, requiring registration after five years, allowing remixes and banning digital locks on legal uses.

Free the sharing of culture and protect private communication.

Defined from: Christian Engström and Rick Falkvinge, The Case for Copyright Reform (2012)
