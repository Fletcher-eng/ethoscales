What is Hoppean Libertarianism?

Hoppean Libertarianism is a political ideology in the Libertarian family. Hoppe argues that democracy is worse than monarchy because elected rulers are temporary caretakers who plunder for the short term. His ideal is no state at all, with all land privately owned. Owners would form covenant communities free to set their own rules and to exclude those who reject them, which gives the theory a strongly conservative social character.

A private law society of covenant communities.

Defined from: Hans-Hermann Hoppe, Democracy: The God That Failed (2001)
