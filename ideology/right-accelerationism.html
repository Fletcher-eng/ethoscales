What is Right Accelerationism?

Right Accelerationism is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. It sees capitalism and technology as one self reinforcing process that is outrunning human control, and holds that this should be sped up, not restrained. Democracy is treated as a brake that consumes the future for present comfort. Land favours exit into competing, non democratic jurisdictions over any attempt at reform.

Capital and technology are an intelligence to be unleashed.

Defined from: Nick Land, Fanged Noumena (2011) and The Dark Enlightenment (2012)
