What is Ecomodernism?

Ecomodernism is a political ideology in the Green family. The manifesto argues that humanity should shrink its impact on the environment by intensifying its activity, not by trying to live in harmony with nature. Cities, intensive farming, desalination and dense energy sources such as nuclear power let people use less land and fewer resources, which leaves more room for wild nature. It rejects the view that growth must stop and holds that modernisation is what lifts people out of poverty. Public investment in clean energy technology is preferred to limits on consumption.

Use technology to decouple human wellbeing from damage to nature.

Defined from: Ted Nordhaus, Michael Shellenberger and sixteen co-authors, An Ecomodernist Manifesto (2015)
