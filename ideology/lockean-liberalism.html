What is Lockean Liberalism?

Lockean Liberalism is a political ideology in the Liberal family. Locke begins from a state of nature in which people are free and equal under a natural law given by God. A person comes to own what they mix their labour with. People agree to form a government only to protect their lives, liberties and estates better than they could alone, so its power is held in trust. A ruler who breaks that trust may rightfully be resisted and replaced by the people.

Government rests on the consent of the governed and exists to protect life, liberty and property.

Defined from: John Locke, Two Treatises of Government (1689)
