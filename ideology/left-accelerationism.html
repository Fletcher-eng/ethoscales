What is Left Accelerationism?

Left Accelerationism is a political ideology in the Left family. It argues the left should stop defending local, small scale resistance and instead take over the technology that capitalism built. Automation, logistics and planning tools should be repurposed toward a world without work. Its demands include full automation, a universal basic income and a shorter working week.

Demand full automation.

Defined from: Nick Srnicek and Alex Williams, #Accelerate Manifesto (2013) and Inventing the Future (2015)
