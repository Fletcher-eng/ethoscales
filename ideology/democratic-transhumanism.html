What is Democratic Transhumanism?

Democratic Transhumanism is a political ideology in the Left family. It holds that people should be free to use technology to extend their lives and abilities, and that democratic states must make sure these benefits are safe and shared. It opposes both bioconservatives who would ban enhancement and libertarians who would leave it to the market. Hughes treats control over one's own body and mind as a civil right.

The right to enhance, with fair access for all.

Defined from: James Hughes, Citizen Cyborg (2004)
