What is Bioregionalism?

Bioregionalism is a political ideology in the Green family. Sale argued that the ecological crisis comes from living at too large a scale, cut off from the places that sustain us. People should come to know the land they inhabit and organise society around bioregions, areas defined by watersheds, soils and living communities and not by political borders. Each region should aim at self sufficiency, with a conserving economy and decisions made in small communities. He expected regions to differ in their political forms, since diversity among them is itself a natural principle.

Political and economic life rescaled to fit natural regions.

Defined from: Kirkpatrick Sale, Dwellers in the Land: The Bioregional Vision (1985)
