What is Eurocommunism?

Eurocommunism is a political ideology in the Radical Left family. Carrillo, then leader of the Communist Party of Spain, argues that in developed Western countries socialism must be reached and kept through elections, several parties, civil liberties and the possibility of being voted out. The Soviet model is not to be copied and no foreign party may give orders. Instead of smashing the state, communists should win over its institutions and democratise them from within, in alliance with other social forces.

A democratic road to socialism, independent of Moscow.

Defined from: Santiago Carrillo, Eurocommunism and the State (1977)
