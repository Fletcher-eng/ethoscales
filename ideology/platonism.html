What is Platonic Guardianship?

Platonic Guardianship is a political ideology in the Classical family. Plato held that justice means each class doing the work it is fit for: producers, soldiers and rulers. The rulers must be philosophers trained for decades to know the good. To keep them from corruption, the guardian class owns no private property and raises its children in common, and poetry and music are censored.

Until philosophers are kings, cities will have no rest from evils.

Defined from: Plato, The Republic (c. 375 BC)
