What is Christian Realism?

Christian Realism is a political ideology in the Religious family. Niebuhr argued that individuals are capable of putting others first, but nations, classes and races act from collective self interest that reason and goodwill cannot dissolve. Justice between groups is therefore won by setting power against power, and some coercion is unavoidable in politics. He criticised both secular and religious liberals for trusting education and moral appeal, and denied that there is a clean line between violent and non violent pressure. The aim is not a perfect society but enough justice to prevent disaster.

Individuals can be unselfish; groups rarely are.

Defined from: Reinhold Niebuhr, Moral Man and Immoral Society (1932)
