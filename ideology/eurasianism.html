What is Neo Eurasianism (Fourth Political Theory)?

Neo Eurasianism (Fourth Political Theory) is a political ideology in the Third Position family. It claims that liberalism, communism and fascism are all exhausted products of Western modernity, and proposes a fourth theory built on tradition and on distinct civilisations. It wants a multipolar world of large land based blocs in place of one global order. Inside such a bloc, each people is meant to keep its own faith and customs under a strong sacral authority.

Against liberalism, communism and fascism alike.

Defined from: Aleksandr Dugin, The Fourth Political Theory (2009)
