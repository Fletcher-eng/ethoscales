What is Insurrectionary Anarchism?

Insurrectionary Anarchism is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. Bonanno argues that revolutionaries have absorbed the values of the system they oppose: work, sacrifice, productivity and organisation as ends in themselves. He rejects waiting for conditions to ripen and rejects formal bodies, including unions, parties and armed vanguard groups, in favour of small groups based on affinity that act directly and at once. Revolt is to be an act of play and joy that destroys work and the commodity, not the self managed running of the same production. An Italian court sentenced him to prison for the book.

Attack now, in small informal groups, without waiting for the right moment.

Defined from: Alfredo M. Bonanno, Armed Joy (1977)
