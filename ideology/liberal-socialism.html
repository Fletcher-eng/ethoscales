What is Liberal Socialism?

Liberal Socialism is a political ideology in the Left family. Written while Rosselli was confined on the island of Lipari by the Fascist regime. He argued that socialism is the heir of liberalism, not its enemy: it aims to make freedom real for people who are too poor to use it. Socialists should drop the Marxist belief that history guarantees their victory and treat socialism as a moral choice. The liberal method of free debate, elections and respect for opponents is binding on them, and the economy should mix private, cooperative and public ownership.

Socialism as liberty carried through to the lives of the poor.

Defined from: Carlo Rosselli, Liberal Socialism (Socialismo liberale) (1930)
