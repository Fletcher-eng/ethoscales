What is Market Socialism?

Market Socialism is a political ideology in the Left family. Lange's reply to Mises: an economy can own its industries in common and still calculate rationally. A planning board sets the prices of capital goods by trial and error, and managers of public firms respond to them as they would in a market. Consumers keep free choice of goods and jobs.

Public ownership, with prices doing the work.

Defined from: Oskar Lange, On the Economic Theory of Socialism (1936)
