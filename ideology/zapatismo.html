Ideologies / Autonomist

What is Zapatismo?

Zapatismo is a political ideology in the Autonomist family. An indigenous movement from Chiapas that does not seek state power. Communities govern themselves through rotating councils whose delegates must obey those who chose them. It defends communal land and indigenous customs against neoliberal globalisation, and aims at a world in which many worlds fit.

To lead by obeying.

Defined from: Zapatista Army of National Liberation, Sixth Declaration of the Lacandon Jungle (2005)
