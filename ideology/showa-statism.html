What is Shōwa Statism?

Shōwa Statism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. Written in 1919, banned and then published in censored form, the plan calls for a coup that suspends the constitution for three years under martial law and unites the emperor directly with the people. Private fortunes, landholdings and firms above fixed limits pass to the state, and workers share in profits. Kita held that Japan, as a poor nation among rich empires, has the right to wage war for territory and to lead Asia. The plan inspired the young officers of the 1936 mutiny.

A coup in the emperor's name, state control of big capital, empire abroad.

Defined from: Kita Ikki, An Outline Plan for the Reorganization of Japan (1923)
