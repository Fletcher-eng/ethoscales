What is Mohism?

Mohism is a political ideology in the Classical family. The Mohists taught that disorder comes from partiality: caring for one's own family and state at the expense of others. The cure is impartial care for everyone, judged by what benefits the people. They condemned offensive war as mass murder while training to defend cities under attack. Office should go to the able whatever their birth, and all should conform their standards to those of their superiors, up to the ruler and to Heaven. They attacked lavish funerals, court music and fatalism as waste.

Impartial care for all, and an end to wars of aggression.

Defined from: Mozi and his school, Mozi (5th to 3rd century BC)
