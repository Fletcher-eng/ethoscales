What is Distributism?

Distributism is a political ideology in the Conservative family. The problem with capitalism is too few capitalists. Belloc and Chesterton held that concentrated ownership, whether by big business or by the state, reduces most people to dependence. Productive property should be spread as widely as possible among families, small farms, workshops and guilds, in line with Catholic social teaching.

Three acres and a cow.

Defined from: Hilaire Belloc and G. K. Chesterton, The Servile State (1912) and The Outline of Sanity (1926)
