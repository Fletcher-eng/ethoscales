What is Morean Utopianism?

Morean Utopianism is a political ideology in the Classical family. More's traveller describes an island commonwealth where all goods are held in common, money is abolished and gold is used for chamber pots. Everyone works six hours a day at farming and a trade, cities are built to one plan, and households elect the magistrates who choose a prince for life. Daily life is closely regulated: dress is uniform, travel needs a permit and serious offenders become slaves. Most religions are tolerated, and war is despised and left to mercenaries where possible. More presents the scheme through a narrator and voices his own doubts about it.

An island with no money and no private property.

Defined from: Thomas More, Utopia (1516)
