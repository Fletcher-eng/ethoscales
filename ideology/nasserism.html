Ideologies / Nationalist

What is Nasserism?

Nasserism is a political ideology in the Nationalist family. Nasser placed Egypt at the centre of three circles: Arab, African and Islamic. The army is the vanguard that frees the nation from foreign control and feudal landowners. The state then drives development through nationalisation and land reform, while staying outside both the American and Soviet camps.

Arab unity, socialism, and no alignment with either bloc.

Defined from: Gamal Abdel Nasser, The Philosophy of the Revolution (1954) and the National Charter (1962)
