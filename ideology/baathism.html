What is Ba'athism?

Ba'athism is a political ideology in the Nationalist family. It calls for the rebirth of a single Arab nation, divided by colonial borders, into one state. Its socialism means state direction of the economy for national development, not class war. It is secular in government while treating Islam as part of Arab cultural heritage, and it organises through a vanguard party.

Unity, freedom, socialism.

Defined from: Michel Aflaq, On the Way of Resurrection (collected essays) (1959)
