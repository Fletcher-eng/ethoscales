What is Dhammic Socialism?

Dhammic Socialism is a political ideology in the Religious family. In essays of the 1970s the Thai monk Buddhadasa argued that socialism is the natural state of things: in nature nothing takes more than its share, and the early Buddhist community lived the same way. Hoarding a surplus is the root of social disorder. He set this against both capitalism and Marxism, which he saw as driven by greed and class revenge. He also distrusted liberal democracy as licence for selfish desire, and preferred firm rule by a leader bound by the ten virtues of a Buddhist king.

Take only what you need; the rest belongs to all.

Defined from: Buddhadasa Bhikkhu, Dhammic Socialism (1986)
