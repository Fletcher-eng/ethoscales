What is Stalinism?

Stalinism is a political ideology in the Radical Left family. Against Trotsky, Stalin argued that a complete socialist society could be built inside the Soviet Union alone, without waiting for revolution abroad. In practice this became forced collectivisation of farming and rapid heavy industry under five year plans. He later added that class struggle sharpens as socialism advances, which justified an ever stronger security state.

Socialism in one country.

Defined from: J. V. Stalin, Concerning Questions of Leninism (1926)
