What is Degrowth?

Degrowth is a political ideology in the Green family. Latouche argued that endless economic growth is impossible on a finite planet and that sustainable development is a contradiction in terms. Rich societies should deliberately shrink production and consumption and free their imagination from the idea that more is always better. He set out a programme of eight linked changes: re-evaluate, reconceptualise, restructure, redistribute, relocalise, reduce, re-use and recycle. In practice this means local economies, shorter working hours and a smaller ecological footprint.

Leave the growth society and live better with less.

Defined from: Serge Latouche, Farewell to Growth (Petit traité de la décroissance sereine) (2007)
