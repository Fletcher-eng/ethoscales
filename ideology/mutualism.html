What is Mutualism?

Mutualism is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. It distinguishes property used to extract rent and interest, which Proudhon called theft, from possession based on personal use, which he defended. Free producers and cooperatives would trade in a market, financed by mutual credit at cost. Society would be held together by contracts and federation instead of a state.

Markets without landlords, bosses or bankers.

Defined from: Pierre-Joseph Proudhon, What Is Property? (1840) and The Principle of Federation (1863)
