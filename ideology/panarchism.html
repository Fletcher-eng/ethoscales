What is Panarchism?

Panarchism is a political ideology in the Libertarian family. De Puydt extends free competition from the economy to government itself. Each person would register with the government they prefer at a local office and could change it later, as they might change a subscription. Monarchies, republics and every other system would exist side by side on the same territory, each ruling only its own members, with disputes between them handled as they are between neighbouring states. Nobody would be forced to live under a system they had not chosen.

Choose your government as you choose your church, without moving house.

Defined from: Paul Émile de Puydt, Panarchy (1860)
