What is Third International Theory?

Third International Theory is a political ideology in the Third Position family. The Green Book rejects parliaments and parties as a fraud on the people and proposes direct rule through popular congresses and committees. In economics it says workers should be partners, not wage earners, and that a house belongs to whoever lives in it. Nation, religion and custom are named as the natural basis of society. This describes the text, not how Libya was actually governed.

Neither capitalism nor communism, but the state of the masses.

Defined from: Muammar Gaddafi, The Green Book (1975 to 1979)
