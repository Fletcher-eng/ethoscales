What is New Left?

New Left is a political ideology in the Left family. Marcuse argued that advanced industrial society, capitalist and Soviet alike, controls people less by force than by satisfying needs that it has itself created. Consumer goods, mass media and technical rationality absorb opposition, so the organised working class no longer acts as a force for change and thought loses its critical dimension. Hope lies in a refusal of the whole system, which he expected to come from outsiders and the excluded. The book became a central text for the student movements of the 1960s.

A refusal of the comfortable unfreedom of affluent industrial society.

Defined from: Herbert Marcuse, One-Dimensional Man (1964)
