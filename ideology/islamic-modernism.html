What is Islamic Modernism?

Islamic Modernism is a political ideology in the Religious family. Iqbal argued that Islam is dynamic at its core and that its law froze only when independent reasoning, ijtihad, was abandoned. He called for that reasoning to be taken up again in the light of modern science and philosophy. The power to interpret the law, he proposed, should pass from individual jurists to an elected Muslim legislative assembly, and he judged the republican form of government to be fully in keeping with the spirit of Islam.

Reopen the gates of independent reasoning.

Defined from: Muhammad Iqbal, The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam (1930)
