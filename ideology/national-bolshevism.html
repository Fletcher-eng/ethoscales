What is National Bolshevism?

National Bolshevism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. Niekisch argued that Germany could only escape the liberal West by adopting a fully planned, state owned economy and aligning with Soviet Russia. The nation, not the international working class, is the subject of the revolution. It is militarised, anti liberal and anti bourgeois, and unlike Falangism it has no religious foundation.

Soviet style planning in the service of the nation.

Defined from: Ernst Niekisch, Widerstand essays and Hitler: A German Fate (1932)
