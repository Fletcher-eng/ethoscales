What is Social Gospel?

Social Gospel is a political ideology in the Religious family. Rauschenbusch argued that the Hebrew prophets and Jesus were concerned with the life of the community, and that the Kingdom of God means the transformation of society, not only the rescue of individual souls. The church lost this purpose over the centuries. Industrial capitalism, built on competition and the wage system, was producing a crisis that Christians had to answer by siding with the working class. He supported trade unions, cooperatives and public ownership of natural monopolies.

The Kingdom of God as a just social order on earth.

Defined from: Walter Rauschenbusch, Christianity and the Social Crisis (1907)
