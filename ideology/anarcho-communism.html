What is Anarcho Communism?

Anarcho Communism is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. It argues that everything produced is the common inheritance of humanity, so both the state and private property should be abolished at once. Free communes would federate voluntarily and share goods according to need, without wages or money. Kropotkin grounded this in mutual aid as a factor of evolution.

All is for all.

Defined from: Peter Kropotkin, The Conquest of Bread (1892)
