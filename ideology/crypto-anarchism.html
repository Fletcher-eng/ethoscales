What is Crypto Anarchism?

Crypto Anarchism is a political ideology in the Libertarian family. It predicts that encryption, anonymous networks and digital cash will let people communicate and trade with total privacy. Once transactions cannot be traced, governments lose the ability to tax, regulate and control information. May saw this as a technical fact that would change society whether or not anyone approved.

Strong cryptography makes the state unable to see or tax.

Defined from: Timothy C. May, The Crypto Anarchist Manifesto (1988)
