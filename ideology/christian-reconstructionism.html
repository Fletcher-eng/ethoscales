What is Christian Reconstructionism (Theonomy)?

Christian Reconstructionism (Theonomy) is a political ideology in the Religious family. Rushdoony held that all law is religious in origin and that the law given in the Old Testament, including its civil and penal provisions, remains binding on every society. The book works through the Ten Commandments as a complete legal order, with capital penalties for offences such as adultery and blasphemy. The civil state is to be small and confined to justice, while most of life is governed by the family, the church and the self governing believer. He regarded democracy and state schooling as rivals to God's sovereignty.

The law of Moses as the standard for every society.

Defined from: Rousas John Rushdoony, The Institutes of Biblical Law (1973)
