What is Black Feminism?

Black Feminism is a political ideology in the Left family. Written by a collective of Black lesbian socialists in Boston. They held that racial, sexual, heterosexual and class oppression operate at the same time in Black women's lives and cannot be separated or ranked. The most radical politics therefore grows out of a group's own identity and experience, an approach the statement named identity politics. Because Black women sit at the bottom of several hierarchies, their freedom would require dismantling all of them. The authors called themselves socialists, rejected separatism, and committed to working without internal hierarchy.

Race, sex, sexuality and class oppression interlock and must be fought together.

Defined from: Combahee River Collective (Demita Frazier, Beverly Smith and Barbara Smith), The Combahee River Collective Statement (1977)
