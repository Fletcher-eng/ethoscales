What is Longtermism?

Longtermism is a political ideology in the Technological family. MacAskill argues that people who will live in the future matter morally as much as people alive now, that they may vastly outnumber us, and that present choices can shape their lives. Improving the prospects of the long term future should therefore rank among the main moral tasks of the present. In practice this means reducing the risk of human extinction or civilisational collapse from engineered pandemics, misaligned artificial intelligence, great power war and climate change, and avoiding any premature and permanent lock in of a single set of values.

Future people count, and there could be a great many of them.

Defined from: William MacAskill, What We Owe the Future (2022)
