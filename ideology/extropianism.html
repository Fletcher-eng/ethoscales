What is Extropianism?

Extropianism is a political ideology in the Technological family. More's principles, first issued in 1990 and revised several times, set out an early and influential form of transhumanism. They call for perpetual progress, the removal of biological limits such as ageing and death, and the continual improvement of the self through science and technology. Reason is preferred to faith or dogma, and optimism about the future is treated as a practical duty. Politically the principles favour an open society, free exchange, individual self direction and decentralised power, and oppose authoritarian control.

No limits on life, intelligence or self transformation.

Defined from: Max More, The Extropian Principles (1990), revised as Principles of Extropy (2003)
