What is Nouvelle Droite (European New Right)?

Nouvelle Droite (European New Right) is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. The manifesto holds that liberal modernity, with its individualism, universalism and worship of the market, is flattening the world's peoples into one mass. It defends the right of every people and culture to remain distinct, opposes large scale immigration as an uprooting of everyone involved, and proposes a federal Europe of regions with local, participatory democracy and respect for ecological limits. Scholars generally class the movement as far right and many read its ethnopluralism as separation of peoples in cultural dress.

The right to difference, against a single world culture.

Defined from: Alain de Benoist and Charles Champetier, Manifesto for a European Renaissance (1999)
