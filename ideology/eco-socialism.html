What is Eco Socialism?

Eco Socialism is a political ideology in the Green family. It argues that the ecological crisis and social breakdown share one cause: an economy that must expand without limit. Neither green capitalism nor the productivist socialism of the Soviet type can solve it. The answer is democratic planning of production around real needs within ecological limits.

Capitalism cannot be made green.

Defined from: Joel Kovel and Michael Löwy, An Ecosocialist Manifesto (2001)
