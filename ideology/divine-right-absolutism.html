What is Divine Right Absolutism?

Divine Right Absolutism is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. Filmer denies that people are born free or that government rests on consent. God gave Adam authority over his family and descendants, and kings hold that same fatherly power by inheritance, so a subject can no more depose a king than a child can choose a father. The king is above the law, which is his own will, and parliaments sit by his grace. Written before the English Civil War and printed in 1680, it was the text Locke set out to refute.

Kings rule as fathers, by the authority God gave Adam.

Defined from: Sir Robert Filmer, Patriarcha, or The Natural Power of Kings (1680)
