What is Revisionist Zionism?

Revisionist Zionism is a political ideology in the Nationalist family. Jabotinsky argued that no native population has ever agreed to be colonised, so the Arabs of Palestine would never consent freely to a Jewish majority. Zionism therefore had either to stop or to go ahead under the protection of a force the Arabs could not break, which he called an iron wall. Only once that wall stood, he held, would moderate Arab leaders negotiate, and he said the two peoples could then live with equal rights.

Settlement behind an iron wall of force.

Defined from: Ze'ev Jabotinsky, The Iron Wall (1923)
