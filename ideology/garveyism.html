What is Garveyism?

Garveyism is a political ideology in the Nationalist family. The declaration lists the wrongs done to black people across the world and then claims their rights as one people: equal treatment before the law, self determination, and the freedom of Africa from colonial rule. Garvey's movement held that these rights would only be secured by race pride, black owned enterprise and a strong independent nation in Africa, and it looked to separate development, not integration.

Africa for the Africans, at home and abroad.

Defined from: Marcus Garvey and the 1920 convention of the Universal Negro Improvement Association, Declaration of the Rights of the Negro Peoples of the World (1920)
