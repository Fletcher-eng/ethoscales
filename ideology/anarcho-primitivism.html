What is Anarcho Primitivism?

Anarcho Primitivism is a political ideology in the Anarchist family. It locates the origin of domination in agriculture, the division of labour and symbolic culture, not just in the state or capitalism. It holds that hunter gatherer life was freer, healthier and more equal. The goal is to undo industrial civilisation and return to small, wild, face to face bands.

Civilisation itself is the problem.

Defined from: John Zerzan, Future Primitive (1994)
