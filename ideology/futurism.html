What is Futurism?

Futurism is a political ideology in the Third Position family. Marinetti demanded the destruction of museums, libraries and every inherited piety, and the worship of speed, machines, youth and danger. The manifesto glorifies war as the world's only hygiene. Its politics were fiercely nationalist and anti clerical, and many Futurists fed into early Fascism while despising its later compromise with tradition.

A racing car is more beautiful than the Victory of Samothrace.

Defined from: F. T. Marinetti, The Founding and Manifesto of Futurism (1909)

Who founded Futurism?

Futurism is defined here from The Founding and Manifesto of Futurism (1909) by F. T. Marinetti. The thinker most tied to it is Filippo Tommaso Marinetti (1876 to 1944).

Is Futurism left wing or right wing?

On a classic two axis compass Futurism falls in the Authoritarian Right quadrant, which means the economic centre and the authoritarian side. Left and right hide a lot, though, which is why EthosScales scores it on thirteen separate axes.
