What is Anabaptist Pacifism?

Anabaptist Pacifism is a political ideology in the Religious family. Seven articles agreed by Swiss Anabaptists. The church is a voluntary body of adult believers who govern themselves, choose their own pastors and discipline members only by exclusion. Believers must separate from the world. The sword is ordained by God to punish the wicked, but it lies outside the perfection of Christ, so a Christian may not bear arms, serve as a magistrate or sit in judgement. Oaths are forbidden. Unlike Tolstoy, the text does not call for the state to be abolished, only for believers to stand apart from it.

A church apart, without sword or oath.

Defined from: Michael Sattler and the Swiss Brethren, The Schleitheim Confession (1527)
