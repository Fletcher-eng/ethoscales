What is Religious Zionism?

Religious Zionism is a political ideology in the Religious family. Kook taught that the Land of Israel is not an external possession of the Jewish people but part of its inner spiritual life, and that Jewish thought and holiness can be whole only there. The national revival is therefore a stage in divine redemption. Even secular pioneers who reject religion are, without knowing it, carrying out a sacred purpose by rebuilding the nation and its land. Unlike Political Zionism, which sought a refuge and a normal state, this makes the return a religious duty.

The return to the Land as the beginning of redemption.

Defined from: Abraham Isaac Kook, Orot (1920)
