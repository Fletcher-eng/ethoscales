What is Radical Traditionalism?

Radical Traditionalism is a political ideology in the Reactionary family. Evola held that history is a long decline from a primordial order ruled by a sacred kingship and a warrior caste. Democracy, equality, materialism and mass nationalism are all symptoms of that fall. He wanted an aristocratic, imperial order founded on spiritual authority, and criticised Fascism for being too modern and too plebeian.

Against the modern world, for hierarchy and the sacred.

Defined from: Julius Evola, Revolt Against the Modern World (1934)
