What is Classical Marxism?

Classical Marxism is a political ideology in the Radical Left family. It reads all recorded history as the history of class struggles, and holds that capitalism, by concentrating industry and creating a propertyless working class, produces the force that will end it. The workers are to win political power, which the text calls winning the battle of democracy, and use it to bring credit, transport and production step by step into public hands. Class distinctions and the state as an instrument of class rule would then fade. It says the workers have no country and that its aims require the forcible overthrow of existing conditions.

Workers of all countries, unite!

Defined from: Karl Marx and Friedrich Engels, Manifesto of the Communist Party (1848)
