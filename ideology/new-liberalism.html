What is New Liberalism?

New Liberalism is a political ideology in the Liberal family. Hobhouse argues that the old liberal fight against arbitrary power is incomplete, because a contract between unequal parties is not truly free. Real liberty needs the state to secure the conditions in which each person can develop: a living wage, education and insurance against sickness, unemployment and old age. Much wealth is created by society and not by its holder, so taxing inheritance and unearned gains is just. He presents this as a development of liberalism, distinct from state socialism.

Liberty without equality is a name of noble sound and squalid result.

Defined from: L. T. Hobhouse, Liberalism (1911)
